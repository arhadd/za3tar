The za3tar method

AI changed how we work.

A small team with the right setup can now do far more than it used to. This page is how we do it: the ideas we work by, and the actual tools underneath.

The ideas

Work where you already work.

The assistant lives in the chat the team already uses: WhatsApp, Slack or Telegram. Nobody installs anything new to start. You ask the way you’d ask a colleague, by text or voice note, and it answers in the same thread.

One agent per company, not one chat per person.

What one person teaches it, the next person gets. The house rules become everyone’s defaults, and what the company knows builds up in one place instead of sitting in someone’s chat history.

One place that connects it all.

Every project has one line on where it stands. Sources, decisions, people and what is waiting on someone all point back to the same place. When a fact changes, it changes there first.

Nothing important goes out without a yes.

The assistant drafts, gathers, chases and builds. Anything that leaves the company, costs money or is hard to undo waits for a person. Every change to its own setup is written up and approved before it goes live.

Small steps, every week.

We don’t redesign how a company works. We take the task that eats the most time, make it run on its own, and move to the next one. After a few months there are dozens of small systems nobody had to scope or wait for.

Build only when the pain is real.

Most of the time the tool you need already exists and only needs connecting. When it doesn’t, a booking page, a report or a small internal tool, we build it, and it joins the setup.

You learn it by using it.

We teach beside the team, on their own work, until they ask for things on their own. The goal is a team that keeps going without us.

It’s yours.

Your data stays in your own tools. You choose the models and can change them. It can run on your own server. It is built on open-source software, so nothing locks you in.

How a setup runs

1Set up

The first weeks with the team: how the work really moves, who asks for what, where the time goes. The assistant goes into the team’s chat on day one.

2Connect

The tools the company already uses are connected, so the assistant works from the real numbers and nobody copies things by hand.

3Maintain

Every connection is checked, every answer is tested against what a good answer looks like, and anything that breaks is fixed before the team notices.

4Grow

The team brings new jobs and new tools, and the setup grows with the company.

The actual setup

The assistant
An always-on agent built on Hermes, an open-source agent framework. Each company gets its own, on its own server or under its own account, with its own memory, rules and skills. It is never shared between companies.
The chat
WhatsApp, Slack or Telegram, whichever the team lives in. Ask it directly or in a group; it can also post on a schedule: a morning brief, a weekly report, a reminder.
The models
We pick the model for the job and can switch at any time, so you are never tied to one provider.
One live map
A single file with one line per thing in motion: what it is, where it stands, what’s next, who owns it. People and agents both read it and both update it.
The za3tar app
Our open-source Mac workspace. Meetings are recorded without a bot in the call, and the decisions and to-dos land in the right project. It shows what the assistant is doing and what is waiting on you. github.com/arhadd/za3tar
Building
When something needs building, we build it with AI coding tools, on the same stack every time: a web app, a Postgres database, and code that lives in the company’s own repository. Every change is a pull request that a person approves.
Approvals and checks
Anything outgoing or hard to undo asks first. Assistants are tested against a set of real questions, and the results go to a person, not just a log.
Secrets
Keys and passwords never go through chat or email. They live in a vault on the server, and nobody pastes them anywhere.
