# Code of conduct

Za3tar is built for teams that switch between Arabic and English mid-sentence,
and the people working on it come from many places and write in more than one
language. Treat each other accordingly.

## What we expect

- Be kind and direct. Criticise the code or the idea, never the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming someone is wrong about their own
  language, dialect or workplace.
- Write issues and reviews in English, Arabic or both. Nobody is corrected for
  their grammar.
- Keep private things private: no real meeting audio, transcripts, contact
  details or keys in issues, pull requests or discussions.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, insults, discrimination on any ground, sexual language or imagery,
publishing someone's private information, and sustained disruption of a
discussion.

## What happens

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, and block people who
ignore this. To report something privately, use the form at
https://github.com/arhadd/za3tar/security/advisories/new and say it is a
conduct report. Reports are read by the maintainer only.

This follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
