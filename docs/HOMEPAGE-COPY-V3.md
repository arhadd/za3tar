## 2. What teams use it for

**Each company ends up with its own operating system.**

**An agency with its own operating system.**
Every client, project, vendor and deadline in one place, from first brief to final invoice. Meetings turn into tasks, the schedule keeps itself, and the weekly report is already written on Monday. The founder stops being where everything meets.

**A design studio that runs its operation from one command line.**
"Shoot the new collection, list it on the store, tell me what's missing." Product imagery, catalogues and store listings move in batches, and the team spends its time on the work only people can do.

**An events company with every department in one place.**
Tickets, guests, the door, crew, rooms, artists and money all live in one place, and every department works from it. Planning a new event starts from everything the last one taught.

## 3. How it works

**The same start for every company. Your team takes it from there.**

1. **Set up.** Your teammate joins your chat, and we bring your first tools into one place.
2. **Maintain.** We keep the tools connected and everything current, so your team never copies anything by hand.
3. **Grow.** Your team brings new jobs and new tools, and the system grows with the company.

Yours to keep: the system, the agents, your data.
