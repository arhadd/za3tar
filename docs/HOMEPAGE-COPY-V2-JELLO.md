## 2. What it is

**One teammate for the whole company.**

It lives in WhatsApp, Slack or Telegram, in English and Arabic. Anyone on the team can ask it something, teach it something or hand it a job.

What one person teaches it, the whole team gets. The way your company works becomes something everyone can use. Over time, you build dozens of useful little systems together, one real job at a time.

---

## 3. What it does in a normal week

Here is what teams are already doing with theirs. Your team will find its own.

**Writes like your company**  
Proposals, updates and replies, written from your templates and the way your team actually speaks. Someone checks and sends.

**Answers from real numbers**  
“What did we spend on events this month?” gets answered from your accounting or booking tool, with the source behind every number.

**Turns meetings into work**  
Notes become tasks with owners and dates. The teammate follows up and keeps the work moving.

**Keeps projects clear**  
Every project gets one living page with the status, people, files and what happens next, updated from your tools.

**Gets Sunday ready**  
It pulls the numbers, drafts the weekly report and puts it in the chat for your team to approve.

**Checks before it acts**  
Money, clients and anything hard to undo always go to a person first. Every action stays visible in the log.

## 5. How it works

**Every company starts in the same place. Your team takes it from there.**

1. **Start.** We put one teammate in your chat and teach it how your company speaks, who does what and the rules your team works by.  
   **You get:** A useful teammate in the place where your team already talks.

2. **Connect.** We connect the tools your company already uses, so the teammate can work with the real files, numbers and project updates.  
   **You get:** Answers and work that come from the real source.

3. **Try.** Your team brings real jobs from a normal week. We try them together, see what works and keep improving them with the people doing the work.  
   **You get:** Useful jobs chosen and shaped by your own team.

4. **Grow.** Every useful job becomes something the team can use again, change and build on. We keep exploring with you through a monthly working session.  
   **You get:** A company that keeps building better ways to work for itself.

The teammate, its instructions and your company data are yours to keep.

- Replaced systems and consulting language with things Ala would actually say out loud: “real jobs,” “the way things get done,” and “one useful job at a time.”

- Made the four steps feel like working together instead of a setup process being done to the company.
