# Za3tar reel — "Meet your new teammate"

~48s, 1080p, silent (the homepage plays it muted). It uses the look of the
agent reels, recoloured in Za3tar's colours (thyme black, limestone, fresh
thyme), with the /demo mascot as the main character. It names no agents or
clients: every project, person and number is made up.

| # | Time | On screen | Beat |

## Why these beats
They're jobs we've actually shipped (project pages, meeting → actions,
numbers from the system of record, approval tiers, Monday digests), not
claims about AI in general. That's the proof the homepage is missing.
